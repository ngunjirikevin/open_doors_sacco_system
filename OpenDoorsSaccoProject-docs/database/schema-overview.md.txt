# Database Schema Overview

## Core Entities
- **members**: stores member bio and membership details
- **staff**: staff users and roles/departments
- **accounts**: BOSA/FOSA/LOAN accounts per member
- **transactions**: deposits, withdrawals, loan payments, fees
- **loans**: loan requests, approvals, repayment tracking
- **receipts**: receipt numbers and transaction references

## Integrity & Relationships
- members -> accounts (1-to-many)
- accounts -> transactions (1-to-many)
- staff -> transactions (recorded_by)
- members -> loans (1-to-many)

## Notes
- Use foreign keys and constraints to maintain integrity
- Store payment references for MPESA tracking and reconciliation
